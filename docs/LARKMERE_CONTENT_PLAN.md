# Larkmere Dense Content Plan

Status: ACTIVE — foundation pass

Larkmere is the first long-term content laboratory. Do not add a second region until this valley demonstrates the full Everdune loop.

- crafting;

## Sleeping Gate

Purpose: optional intensity and mastery.

Activities:
- combat;
- challenge runs;
- enemy knowledge;
- future Gauntlet.

The Gate must remain optional so the player who wants a quiet evening is never pushed into combat.

## Content quality gate

No new location is complete until it has:
- a clear fantasy;
- at least two repeatable activities;
- at least one discovery;
- at least one NPC connection;
- at least one world consequence;
- at least one reason to return;
- authored environmental storytelling;
- a meaningful relationship to Hearthsong.
