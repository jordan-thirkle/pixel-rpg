# EVERDUNE GENESIS

## The Hook

**The World Remembers.**

Everdune is a quiet frontier where an ancient living force called the Hearthsong once connected places, people and nature through memory.

After an event known as **The Quieting**, that memory faded.

The player arrives in Larkmere Valley and gradually discovers that the world can remember again.

Repairing, restoring, exploring, befriending and discovering can awaken **Echoes**: memories left behind by places, people, creatures and objects.

This is the foundation of the game identity.

## Player fantasy

The player is a **wayfarer**, not a chosen one.

They may fish, farm, craft, build, explore, fight, collect, study, decorate, befriend or simply wander.

Quiet play is a first-class playstyle.

## Opening mystery

The player arrives because of an old travelling letter promising them a home.

The sender is apparently dead.

The house is gone.

A sealed stone marker remains, carrying the player's family name.

## Magic

Magic is expressed through Echoes rather than conventional spell lists.

An Echo is a memory anchored to the world.

Examples:
- a bridge remembers the people who built it
- a forest remembers a forgotten path
- an abandoned home remembers its family
- a sword remembers its owner
- a poisoned spring remembers its cause

## Tone

Quiet wonder, warmth, mystery, humour, melancholy without misery, adventure without constant urgency.

## Non-negotiables

- No direct cloning of existing games

- No forced optimisation
