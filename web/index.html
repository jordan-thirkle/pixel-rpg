A WORLD TO CALL HOME

Everdune is a land of quiet beauty and hidden wonders. A place where the old world still whispers beneath the soil, where small lives and great destinies intertwine, and where even the most ordinary day can lead to an extraordinary discovery.

EXPLORE A LIVING WORLD

Wander through forests, mountains, rivers, ruins and forgotten places. Discover secrets around every corner.

GATHER, CRAFT, BUILD

Collect resources, craft useful items, cook meals, build and decorate your home, and create a life that feels yours.

MEET MEMORABLE PEOPLE

Befriend a cast of unique characters, each with their own stories, daily lives and place in the world.

UNCOVER A DEEPER MYSTERY

Everdune hides an ancient truth. As you explore, gather and help others, the world slowly reveals its forgotten history.

The World

Larkmere Valley is the first corner of Everdune: Hearthfall, Silverrun, Briarwood, the Old Road, Bellroot Mine, the Glass Orchard, the Hollow Steps and the Sleeping Gate, all waiting to be remembered.

The People

Mara Vale

Keeper of Hearthfall’s daily rhythm and the first person to notice that you are listening to the valley.

Rowan Bell

A Wayfinder who studies old road-stones and the memories embedded inside them.

The Ferryman

A quiet figure who seems to know where the oldest paths disappear.

Creatures

The valley is populated by ordinary wildlife, strange Echo-born phenomena and optional threats guarding forgotten places.

Locations

Explore Hearthfall, Silverrun, Briarwood, the Old Road, Bellroot Mine, the Glass Orchard, the Hollow Steps and the Sleeping Gate.

Factions

Hearthkeepers, Wayfinders, Ashen Circle and Greenwardens approach the returning memory of Everdune from very different directions.
