A WORLD THAT REMEMBERS

The Hearthsong once connected places, people and nature through memory. After the Quieting, that memory faded. You arrive in Larkmere Valley and discover that ordinary places can still whisper.

Larkmere Valley

FIRST REGION • THE FRONTIER

A compact region designed to feel authored rather than endless. Hearthfall is home; Silverrun carries the valley’s water; Briarwood closes around old paths; Bellroot Mine descends into the forgotten; the Glass Orchard and Hollow Steps hide stranger memories; and the Sleeping Gate waits beyond the Old Road.

Creatures

ORDINARY • STRANGE • DANGEROUS

Wildlife belongs to the world first. Threats are contextual. Echo-born phenomena can be beautiful, unsettling or useful. Combat is optional outside places where you deliberately choose to seek danger.

Development

BUILT IN THE OPEN

Everdune is being built as a real Godot game with data-driven systems, automated validation and a deliberately ruthless vertical-slice quality gate. The companion site mirrors the game’s canon rather than inventing a separate marketing universe.

ENGINE

Godot 4.7.2-stable • native 2D • GDScript • TileMapLayer/TileSet.

CORE IDEA

The World Remembers — exploration, restoration, relationships and Echoes form the spine.

RELEASE

Premium single-player Steam RPG first; future co-op remains an architectural possibility, not a promise.
